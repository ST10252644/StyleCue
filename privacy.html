Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Style Board ("we", "our", "the app") is a personal project for discovering style and fashion inspiration. This policy explains what information is collected and how it is used.

Information We Collect

We do not require account creation. If the app connects to third-party services (such as Pinterest) for content search, we may temporarily access data returned by that service's API in order to display it within the app, such as public image URLs and related metadata.

How We Use Information

  • To display search results and images requested by the user
  • We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties
  • We do not use collected data for advertising

Third-Party Services

This app may use APIs from services such as Pinterest, Unsplash, or Pexels to retrieve images. Use of these services is subject to their own respective privacy policies and terms.

Data Storage

We do not store personal data on our servers beyond what is necessary for the app to function during a session.

Contact

For questions about this policy, please contact the site owner via the GitHub repository associated with this project.

Think it over

A short list of pieces you like but are not sure about. There is no timer, so take as long as you like.

How it works
  1. Save a piece from Picks with "Save for later".
  2. Tick the three quick questions on its card, for example whether you already own something similar.
  3. When all three are ticked, choose "I have decided". You can also choose "Skip it" if you change your mind.

The idea is to pause before you buy, so you only keep what you really want.
